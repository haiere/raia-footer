Privacy Rules

Version 4.0.2 · November 13, 2026

In short: we only collect what we need, and only what you give us yourself. We never sell your data.

1. What data do we collect?

  • What you type yourself, such as your name and message in the contact form.
  • Technical info without names, such as your browser and device, from Google Analytics. This cannot identify you.
  • Your choices, such as language and theme. They are saved in your own browser (localStorage), not on our server.
  • Digital tools, such as HajirSync, Raia Delta, Scrub, Vault, Minify, QRe, and RAIA. They run on your own device, and nothing is sent anywhere.

2. Cookies and other services

  • Function cookies remember your language and theme when you come back.
  • Google Analytics hides your IP address. It is only used to see general visit numbers.
  • Formspree handles the contact form. Messages are deleted at most 30 days after sending.
  • Links to other sites, such as YouTube, Instagram, SoundCloud, and GitHub. Each one has its own privacy rules.

3. How is your data kept safe?

  • This site uses HTTPS/TLS. Every connection is encrypted, so others cannot read it.
  • A Content Security Policy (CSP) adds a layer of protection against attacks.
  • Everything you send in is checked carefully.
  • Every tool runs in your browser, so your data stays on your device.

4. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • See a copy of the personal data we keep.
  • Fix anything that is wrong.
  • Delete your personal data.

Want to ask for one of these? Reach us through the contact form on the main page, or email isthajir@proton.me.

5. If things change

If we change these rules, the date at the top of this page will change too. We will never change them in secret.

6. Contact us

Questions about privacy? Send us a message through the contact form on the main page, or email isthajir@proton.me.

In short: Your data is safe. We only collect what we need. We never sell anything. All tools work on your own device.

Cookie Rules

Version 4.0.2 · November 13, 2026

We use cookies to make this site more comfortable for you. Here is the simple version.

1. What is a cookie?

A cookie is a small file saved on your device when you open a site. It works like a short note. It helps the site remember your choices, such as theme or language, when you come back.

2. Cookies we use

Name Type Purpose Duration
raia_state_v1 Functional Saves your RAIA settings (provider, model, parameters) Until deleted
haiere_lang Preference Saves your language choice (ID/EN) Until deleted
theme Preference Saves your theme choice (light or dark) Until deleted
haiere-cookie Functional Saves your answer to the cookie banner Until deleted
_ga Analytics Tells visitors apart without names 2 years
_ga_* Analytics Saves session state and grouped numbers 2 years

3. Other services

  • Google Analytics shows visit stats with data that has no names.
  • Formspree handles the contact form. It sets no tracking cookies.
  • YouTube, Instagram, SoundCloud, GitHub are outside links. Each one has its own cookie rules.

4. How to manage cookies

  • In your browser: you can refuse or delete cookies in your privacy settings.
  • In the cookie banner: choose accept or decline on your first visit.
  • Opt out of Google Analytics: install the official add-on if you do not want to be tracked.

5. If the rules change

These cookie rules can be updated at any time. The newest version is always on this page.

6. Contact us

Questions about cookies? Send us a message through the contact form on the main page, or email isthajir@proton.me.

In short: Cookies are only used for your comfort, to remember your theme, your language, and anonymous stats. You can manage them yourself at any time.
