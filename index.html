Privacy Rules

Version 4.0.0 — October 10, 2026

In short: we only take what we really need. And only if you give it yourself. Your data is never sold to anyone.

1. What data do we take?

  • What you type yourself — like your name and message in the contact form.
  • Technical info without names — like your browser type and device, from Google Analytics. This cannot identify you.
  • Your choices — language and theme are saved in your own browser (localStorage), not on our server.
  • Digital tools — like HajirSync, Raia Delta, Scrub, Vault, Minify, and RAIA. They all run on your own device. Nothing is sent anywhere.

2. Cookies & other services

  • Function cookies — so your choices (language, theme) are remembered when you come back.
  • Google Analytics — the IP is hidden. Only used to see general visit numbers.
  • Formspree — handles the contact form. Messages are deleted at most 30 days after sending.
  • Links to other sites — like YouTube, Instagram, SoundCloud, and GitHub. Each has its own privacy rules.

3. How is data kept safe?

  • This site uses HTTPS/TLS. That means every connection to this site is locked, so no one else can read it.
  • There is a Content Security Policy (CSP). It is a safety layer against digital attacks.
  • Everything you send in is checked carefully.
  • Every tool runs in your browser. Your data stays on your device.

4. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • See — ask for a copy of the personal data we keep.
  • Fix — ask us to correct anything that is wrong.
  • Delete — ask us to delete your personal data.

Want to ask for one of these? Reach us through the contact form on the main page.

5. If things change

If we change these rules, the "Last updated" date will always be at the top. We will never change rules in secret.

6. Contact us

Questions about privacy? Send us a message through the contact form on the main page.

In short: Your data is safe. We only take what we need. We never sell anything. All tools work on your own device.

Cookie Rules

Version 4.0.0 — October 10, 2026

We use cookies to make this site nicer for you. Here is the simple explanation.

1. What is a cookie?

A cookie is a small file saved on your device when you open a site. It works like a little note. It helps the site remember your choices (like theme or language) when you come back.

2. Cookies we use

Name Type Purpose Duration
raia_state_v1 Functional Saves your RAIA settings (provider, model, parameters) Until deleted
haiere_lang Preference Saves your language choice (ID/EN) Until deleted
theme Preference Saves your theme choice (light/dark) Until deleted
haiere-cookie Functional Saves your answer to the cookie banner Until deleted
_ga Analytics Tells visitors apart without names 2 years
_ga_* Analytics Saves session state & grouped numbers 2 years

3. Other services

  • Google Analytics — shows visit stats with data that has no names.
  • Formspree — handles the contact form (no tracking cookies).
  • YouTube, Instagram, SoundCloud, GitHub — outside links. Each has its own cookie rules.

4. How to manage cookies

  • In your browser — you can refuse or delete cookies in your browser privacy settings.
  • In the cookie banner — pick accept or decline on your first visit.
  • Say no to Google Analytics — install the official add-on if you do not want to be tracked.

5. If the rules change

The cookie rules can be updated at any time. The newest version is always on this page.

6. Contact us

Questions about cookies? Send us a message through the contact form on the main page.

In short: Cookies are only used for your comfort. To remember theme, language, and nameless stats. You can always manage them yourself.
